## Visual & Audio Storyboard

| Timestamp | Visual Screen Action | Narration Script |

In high-volume digital payments, customer transactions are captured instantaneously on the merchant platform, while bank clearing and settlement follow a 1 to 2 business day delay. At month-end close, finance teams face persistent ledger discrepancies due to timing cutoffs, rounding quirks, duplicate settlements, and orphan refunds. This system automates the audit of those gaps using strict decimal precision."* |

Our top KPI cards display total platform volume, settled bank volume, cleared transactions, and our exact Net Reconciliation Gap in Indian Rupees."* |
| **1:00 – 1:45**<br>(45s) | Navigate to Section 1 & Section 2:<br>Show the 4 Anomaly Cards, then open Section 5: **Exception Investigation** | *"Notice how the system isolates the four mandatory anomaly scenarios without manual intervention:*<br>• **1. Late Settlement:** *Two transactions initiated on January 31st settled on February 1st. The system recognizes this as an in-transit timing difference rather than a permanent loss.*<br>• **2. Rounding Variance:** *Five transactions exhibit micro-variances of 1 to 3 paise, accumulating to a measurable net difference of 5 paise. Exact Decimal arithmetic ensures these are routed to a rounding ledger rather than flagged as catastrophic failures.*<br>• **3. Duplicate Settlement:** *Transaction TXN1124 was settled twice by the bank. The duplicate credit of ₹9,500 is quarantined and prevented from inflating revenue.*<br>• **4. Orphan Refund:** *A bank refund debit of ₹2,400 references a non-existent transaction ID, immediately flagged for fraud and operational investigation."* |

Thank you."* |
